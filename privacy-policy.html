Privacy Policy

How we handle your information · Effective 21 October 2026

NK Food Agro uses information you provide to process orders, coordinate delivery, support your account and respond to customer requests.

Information collected

When you place an order, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, PIN code, order items, order total and order notes. If you sign in, the authentication provider may provide account details needed to identify your account.

How information is used

  • To receive, confirm, prepare and deliver orders.
  • To show order history and status in your signed-in account.
  • To contact you about delivery, support or an order issue.
  • To protect the service and maintain order records.

Service providers

Order information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, including Supabase for account/order storage and SheetDB for the order spreadsheet. These services process data under their own technical systems and policies.

Data sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Order details may be shared with delivery or service partners only as needed to fulfil an order, provide support, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention and security

We retain records as needed for order handling, support, business records and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with an order. To request help, email care@nkfoodagro.com and include enough information to identify the relevant order. Do not email passwords or payment credentials.

Children

This store is intended for people who can legally place an order. A parent or guardian should handle purchases for minors.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices or requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
