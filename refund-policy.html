Returns & Refunds

Returns, replacements and refunds · Effective 21 October 2026

Food products require careful handling and hygiene. Please inspect your parcel when it arrives and contact us promptly if something is wrong.

Damaged, missing or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, is missing, or differs from the item ordered, email care@nkfoodagro.com within 48 hours of delivery. Include your order reference, a description of the issue and clear photos of the parcel and product so our team can review it.

Change of mind

For food and consumable products, returns may not be accepted after delivery for hygiene and safety reasons. Please contact us before returning any item; do not send a parcel back without instructions from our team.

Review and resolution

After reviewing the details, we may offer a replacement, refund or another appropriate resolution where applicable. Approval depends on the circumstances and the information provided.

Refund timing

If a refund is approved, our team will confirm the method and expected processing time with you. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary. For Cash on Delivery orders, we will contact you to arrange an appropriate refund method.

Order cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation. A cancellation may not be possible after an order has been dispatched.

Contact

Email care@nkfoodagro.com and include your order reference.
