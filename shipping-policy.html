Shipping Policy

How shipping works · Effective 21 October 2026

NK Food Agro aims to pack every order carefully and deliver it to the address entered at checkout.

Order processing

Orders are reviewed after submission. Our team may contact you to confirm the delivery address or Cash on Delivery details before dispatch.

Delivery estimates

Delivery timing depends on the destination, courier availability and service conditions. If a delivery estimate is provided during order confirmation, that estimate applies. Delays may occur due to weather, public holidays or courier disruptions.

Shipping charges

Any applicable shipping charge will be shown or communicated before the order is confirmed. Do not assume a charge is waived unless it is stated at checkout or confirmed by our team.

Address and delivery

Please provide a complete address, correct PIN code and reachable phone number. If a parcel cannot be delivered because the address or contact details are incorrect, our team may contact you to arrange the next step.

Need help?

Email care@nkfoodagro.com with your order reference.
